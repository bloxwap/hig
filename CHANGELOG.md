# Changelog

## Unreleased

- New block `LanguageCard` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/language-card`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/language-card`, and the `blocks` aggregates): choose the app's language. A header with Back and the title, a search field that filters by name, description and code, then each language as a row with round flag artwork, its own name over the reader's name for it, and a check on the one in use. New message keys `language` and `searchLanguages`.
- New block `TargetCard` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/target-card`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/target-card`, and the `blocks` aggregates): set a take profit or a stop loss on one position. `kind` is `take-profit` (positive role) or `stop-loss` (negative role). Under the shared header it shows the target as the headline figure in the chosen unit (dollars, price or percent), a price card titled with the position and the order type whose bar places the entry, mark and trigger prices on one axis and tints the distance left to the trigger, an optional note, the unit as a `SegmentedControl`, a Custom row, the host's quick choices as capsules, and Cancel beside the confirming action. Every trading word comes from the host through `labels`. `targetLayout` exposes the bar positions and `targetFormat` the number format of each unit. The prices sit directly on the screen; `surface` draws them inside a card. With `labels.info`, `note` opens in a popover from an info button on the trailing edge of the header.
- New component `BarPnl` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/bar-pnl`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/bar-pnl`): a position's profit or loss as one bar. The stretch between entry and mark is tinted `positive` in profit and `negative` at a loss, a tick marks each price, and the liquidation, mark and entry prices read under the bar in price order, so mark and entry trade places as the mark crosses the entry. `labels` is host text for the three prices; `side`, `title`, `description` and `priceOptions` are optional, `compact` shortens the prices for narrow screens, and `surface={false}` draws it without a card. `barPnlLayout` exposes the positions, order and result.
- New components `ListGroup` and `ListRow` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/list-group`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/list-group`), in Navigation: a grouped list for menus and settings. A group has an optional `title` and one rounded container; a row has a `label`, an optional `value`, `leading` and `trailing` content, and with `onSelect` (or `href` on web) becomes one target with a disclosure chevron.
- Breaking: the blocks `BalanceCard`, `CodeWindow` and `ActivityList` are removed (web and native), with their entries `@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/<name>`, the deprecated `@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/<name>` aliases, their catalog pages and examples, and the `noActivity` message key. Use `AccountCard` for balances; for code, a `Surface` around `<pre className='hig-code'>` on web; for activity, `List` with its `empty` text.
- SwapPanel `note` with `noteTone='warning'` or `'negative'` tints at 10% instead of 14% (web and native), the same as Alert. On a surface or sheet the negative note was 4.44:1 against its own tint; it is now above the 4.5:1 text minimum on the page, a surface and an overlay.
- Recipes are now called blocks. The docs chapter, the catalog family and the import paths use the new name: `@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks`, `@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/<name>` and `@bloxwap/hig/native/blocks`. The published `recipes` paths (`@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes`, `@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/<name>`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/recipes`) resolve to the same modules and are deprecated; move imports to `blocks`. They are removed in the next major release. Guide links to `components/recipes` anchors are recorded as renamed to `components/blocks`.
- New block `ManagePositionCard` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/manage-position-card`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/manage-position-card`, and the `blocks` aggregates): the screen for one open position. A `BarMark` titled with the margin mode shows margin against notional and reads the share left before liquidation; a `BarPnl` places the liquidation, mark and entry prices. Both are drawn without a card because they are read-only. Under them, the host's actions as rows with chevrons and the position row with its profit and loss. Every label comes from the host through `labels`, because built-in strings carry no trading terms. `positionCorridor` exposes the share left before liquidation.
- `BarMark` gains `surface={false}`, which draws no card around the bar, matching `BarPnl`. `TargetCard` takes the same `surface` setting for its prices, off by default.
- New block `TokenActionCard` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/token-action-card`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/token-action-card`, and the `blocks` aggregates): the screen for one token. A header with Back and the ticker, an assets `BarMark` (the token name, the total in dollars, and where it is held), a chains `BarMark` with the number of chains that hold a balance, then the actions as equal full-width buttons. `send`, `receive`, `stake` and `unstake` have built-in labels; other actions bring their own. New message keys `receive`, `stake` and `unstake`. `BarMark` gains `size='small'`.
- New block `AccountCard` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/blocks/account-card`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/account-card`, and the `blocks` aggregates) and new component `AssetBalanceRow` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/asset-balance-row`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/asset-balance-row`). The card shows a header (Back, centred title, optional trailing action), the total on the leading edge with a Deposit action on the trailing edge, the pinned dollar balance (`pinnedSymbol`, default `USDC`, always first and always shown, $0.00 when not held), then every other holding by dollar value, largest first. Each holding is an `AssetBalanceRow`: artwork, name over symbol, value over units, in a capsule row 8 apart. All figures update place by place. `onShowAddress` adds a QR button on the trailing edge of the header, above Deposit; the host shows the account address. New message keys `account`, `deposit`, `accountBalance` and `showAddress`.
- New component `BarMark` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/bar-mark`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/bar-mark`): a one-dimensional stacked bar for parts of a whole. A card with a `title`, a `description` on the trailing edge, one rounded bar divided among its `elements` (`{label, value, tone, color, detail}`), and a legend of colour dots and labels. `total` leaves the remainder as empty track. Elements take the `positive`, `info`, `warning`, `negative`, `muted` and `faint` roles in order unless they name a `tone` or a host `color`. The layout follows the writing direction, and each legend item reads its share as a percent.
- Breaking: `AmountInput` is removed from `@bloxwap/hig/react/field` and `@bloxwap/hig/native/field`, with its catalog page and examples. Use `Input` with `type='text' inputMode='decimal'` on web or `keyboardType='decimal-pad'` on native; the rendered input is the same. `TradeEntry` does this itself and is unchanged.

## 0.5.2

SwapPanel takes the last host rules that kept apps on their own amount sheets (web and native).

- `actionDisabled` holds the action for a host reason and `actionStatus` names it; the panel's own prompts (a token, an amount, the balance) still come first. `actionIntent='destructive'` draws the action as destructive.
- `SwapToken.decimals` caps the decimal places typed in token units. `limitToBalance` refuses a key that would pass the From balance.
- `SwapToken.icon`, `SwapToken.chainIcon` and `SwapDestination.icon` take an image URL or a host element (`SwapArtwork`).
- `banner` renders above the From card and `footer` under the action.
- Native: `SwapKeypad` and `SwapCapsule` are exported from `@bloxwap/hig/native/recipes`, as the web recipe already exports its keypad and pill.
- Swap card live numbers (web and native). New requirement in the recipes chapter: every number in the swap card that can change while it is on screen updates through `AnimatedNumberReadout`, moving only the changed places. `SwapPanel` now does this for the From balance, the fee and slippage on the To card, the settings rows and fee breakdown, and the picker balances (`FromPicker`, `ToPicker`), as it already did for the amounts and the conversion capsule. Hosts can pass live figures for their own text: `SwapDetail.value` accepts a number with `options`, `prefix` and `suffix`, and `SwapToken.caption` and `SwapDestination.detail` accept a `SwapFigure` (`{value, options, prefix, suffix}`). Strings still work and stay static. Web exports `SwapNumber` from the swap panel entry for host-drawn parts. Loading and unknown fees stay plain `…` and `—`; reduced motion updates without movement. Native: the conversion capsule now animates too, and `@bloxwap/hig/native/pickers` imports the animated readout, so it needs the optional Reanimated and Worklets peers as the swap panel already did.

## 0.5.0

Design review pass over all 65 catalog components (web and native). HIG now separates layers with fill and space, not strokes.

- Alert and Callout lose the glowing stroke (web and native): severity now tints the fill (the surface mixed with 12% of the tone) and colours the title, at the Surface radius with no border or shadow. `default` is a 6% `text` tint with the title in `text`. Hosts that restyled the 2px border or glow can delete those overrides.
- Tabs no longer use the action colour (web and native): the selected tab is a raised neutral capsule (`--hig-fill-raised`, `useFills().raised`) with `text`, and unselected tabs are bare `muted` labels, matching Navigation and SegmentedControl. Web selects them by `.hig-tab` instead of `[role='tab']`.
- Web: a comfortable density scope nested inside a compact one (a nested `HIGProvider density='comfortable'`, or `data-hig-density='comfortable'` on a wrapper) now restores the 44/48px control sizes and 12px gap; it used to stay compact.
- Visual balance pass (web and native). Thin tracks and rules follow the container like wells do: the Switch off track, Slider and Progress tracks, Spinner ring, Skeleton and Separator take the raised fill (`--hig-fill-raised`, `useFills().raised`), which is `nested` on the page and `border` inside a Surface, Dialog or sheet. On the page they were `border`, the lightest grey, and now sit level with a secondary button; Progress inside a card steps up from `nested` so it no longer matches an input. The platform guide states the rule.
- Disabled Button and IconButton are neutral instead of a half-opacity accent: a well fill with `muted` text for every intent, and no fill with `faint` text for ghost. Busy keeps the intent colour at 70% opacity under its spinner. The SwapPanel action row drops its own disabled override for the shared rule.
- Web: the Menu highlight and press step lighter (`border`) like Select options, instead of darker. Checkbox, Radio and Switch start on the content edge (no 8px leading inset; 16px trailing keeps neighbours apart) and group legends lose the browser's 2px inset. MarketRow in action mode draws the name in `text` and insets its trailing edge 20px to match the name.
- No resting strokes. Surface, Card, inputs, Select, Dialog, Sheet, Menu, Popover, Table and EmptyState lose their border. Control wells (inputs, segmented track, Badge, Avatar) take `surface` on the page and step to `nested` inside a surface or overlay; focus and invalid states keep a ring. Web keeps a transparent 1px border in the box, so sizes are unchanged and forced-colors still draws an edge.
- New colour role `overlay` (`#111111`, `--hig-overlay`): Dialog, AlertDialog, Sheet and GestureSheet panels use it, darker than a card, with a deeper shadow.
- Sheet handle: the GestureSheet drag handle is the action colour, so it follows `primaryColor`. Web draws it 100 × 6px with a soft glow in the same colour; native paints the indicator in `action`. Hosts can delete their own green handle styles.
- Form spacing rule in the platform guide: controls sit 8 apart in both directions, the bottom action row sits 16 below the content above it, and list rows that lead with an avatar are concentric capsules 8 apart.
- Web text fields are 44px high on touch density (36 compact), the same as buttons, down from 48. SearchInput text starts 42px in, down from 46.
- Button and IconButton: busy shows a leading spinner (in place of the icon on IconButton). The busy text is no longer drawn; it stays in the accessible name.
- SegmentedControl is inverted (dark track, lighter selected segment) and 44px high on touch density, 36px compact. Switch: the off track is dark grey.
- SearchInput and Search inset a magnifying glass on the leading edge. Web wraps the input in `span.hig-search-input`; the ref, class and props still reach the input. `SearchInput` defaults to `type="search"` and a host-supplied `type` now wins, so a host can keep a plain text field (no Safari search chrome) inside the HIG anatomy.
- Toast notices are single-row capsules: severity symbol, message, optional action, close symbol. The Dismiss wording moves to the close button's accessible name. Web markup changes from Alert sections to `.hig-toast-item`.
- New web entry `@bloxwap/hig/react/toast` (`Toaster`, `toast()`, `dismissToast()`) raises the capsule through Sonner; `sonner ^2.0.0` is a new optional peer. While a `Toaster` is mounted, `InstallCommand` reports its copy result as a toast; without one it shows the result inline as before. Only the toast entry loads Sonner.
- EmptyState is a centred stack under a default symbol. Web gains `illustration` (as on native) and `description` becomes optional.
- Accordion: outline chevron on the trailing edge that turns as the panel opens; the panel height eases where the browser can interpolate to `auto`. The whole header row is the target.
- Skeleton pulses, and StatusIndicator emits a ring in its status colour for `online` and `stale`. Both stop under reduced motion. Skeleton now fills its container width.
- Separator is a 2px line with rounded ends. Table separates sections with space and type weight instead of rules.
- Native: `useFills()` and `Raised` from `@bloxwap/hig/native/provider` expose the same page/raised fills for host-drawn controls; Button, IconButton and Tabs are 44dp with hit slop keeping a 48dp target; icons are drawn from plain Views, so `react-native-svg` stays optional. `InstallCommand` shows its copy result as a transient toast capsule (4s).
- New recipe `SwapPanel` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/swap-panel`, also in the `recipes` aggregates on web and native): send, swap and bridge in one panel, modelled on the swap product. From and To cards with a flip control, an amount in dollars or token units with a conversion capsule, token pickers (search, chain filter, Hot section) and settings (denomination, slippage, fee breakdown) pushed as views inside the panel, an on-screen keypad, a receive view, a `oneWay` mode that shows a down arrow instead of the flip control, and `denominationSwitch={false}` to fix amounts to one denomination and hide the conversion capsule. For flows beyond token-to-token it takes a fixed `destination` (a compact To card for a recipient wallet, staking pool or account), `lockFrom` / `lockTo`, and an `actionLabel`; a mounted `amount` seeds the entry. It also presents a token with a `caption` as an account, keeps the action disabled while `destination.pending`, shows `details` rows on the bottom card, and takes a `note`, a `title` with `onBack`, `controls={false}` and an `actionIcon`. The catalog example has one canonical state per flow: swap, bridge, send, deposit, withdraw, withdraw to another chain, stake, unstake. Catalog examples may carry per-state `variants`. `SwapSheet` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/swap-sheet`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/swap-sheet`) presents it in a gesture sheet, with the views pushing inside that one sheet. Fully host-driven: the host supplies tokens, balances, prices, the quote and the wallet, and executes on `onSubmit(action)`. Adds 43 `swap*` message keys. The docs stylesheet budget rises from 125,000 to 150,000 bytes for the new component styles.
- `SwapAmountCard` (web, from `@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/swap-panel`): the swap From card on its own, for a host that enters a single amount such as a position size. `SwapPill` and `SwapKeypad` are exported from the same entry.
- New recipes `FromPicker` and `ToPicker` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/recipes/pickers`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/pickers`, and the `recipes` aggregates): the swap panel's source and destination pickers on their own. `ToPicker` is a destination address field with a scan button, alone or above a searchable token list with or without the chain filter. `FromPicker` shows a connected wallet with an optional disconnect button, or Connect wallet and a Generate address switch, above the tokens a wallet holds or a whitelist. `SwapPanel` uses both for its pushed views; its generate-address control is now that switch and no longer leaves the picker. `swapReceiveToAddress` is renamed `swapGenerateAddress`, and the token search now reads “Search name or paste address”.
- New `Filter` in the selection entries (web and native): one row of filter capsules that scrolls sideways, single choice, with a check that draws into the chosen capsule's circle. The swap token picker uses it for chains.
- Floating lists (Select, Menu, Popover, Tooltip) present with a short fade and settle. The Switch track eases with the thumb; both stop under reduced motion, like every other transition. The native Switch on web draws the HIG capsule instead of react-native-web's Material switch. The native Switch bundle budget rises from 2,200 to 2,400 bytes gzipped for it.
- New `VerticalAddress` (data entries) and `WalletAddress` (`@bloxwap/hig/react/wallet-address`, `@bloxwap/hig/native/wallet-address`) for Ethereum-style and Solana addresses: a Cuer QR code beside the address in fixed-width rows with the leading row and last four characters emphasised, and a Tap to copy hint when the host passes `onCopy`. `cuer` is a new optional peer. The SwapPanel receive view uses `VerticalAddress`; its two address message keys are now the shared `copyAddress` and `addressCopied`, with new `tapToCopy` and `qrCode`.
- Side-by-side actions are 8 units apart at every density through a new `--hig-action-gap` variable: Tabs and Pagination move from 12 to 8 on touch density; Alert actions, Filter and the SwapPanel rows already were.
- Closing call-to-action buttons sit a double step, 16 units, below the form and 8 from each other (`--hig-action-offset`, `.hig-form-action`): TradeEntry, the SwapPanel action row, and the buttons that end a Dialog or default sheet.
- The brand mark's copied-palette drift exception, expired on 2026-11-13, is renewed to 2027-05-12 and tracked in #201, so `npm run check` passes again.
- Examples: the IconButton example passes `busy`, and the Tooltip example confirms its copy with a toast.
- Accept the latest feedback and chart packages: the optional peers are now `@bloxwap/sfx` `^0.2.0 || ^0.3.0` and `@bloxwap/haptic` `^0.2.0 || ^0.3.0 || ^0.4.0`, and the required peer is `@bloxwap/chart` `^0.3.0 || ^0.4.0 || ^0.5.0`. HIG develops, smoke-tests and documents against chart 0.5.0, haptic 0.4.0 and sfx 0.3.0; PR CI still checks the oldest accepted versions. Native cues are re-exported with sfx 0.3.0 and are byte-identical.

## 0.3.0

- Every JS export now has a `default` condition beside `import`. Jest/jest-expo, `require()` and Metro's CommonJS resolution can import HIG entries instead of failing with `ERR_PACKAGE_PATH_NOT_EXPORTED`.
- `@bloxwap/hig/react/css` is touch-safe. Every `:hover` rule now sits behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so taps no longer leave buttons dimmed or ghost buttons filled. Hosts can delete their sticky-hover overrides, and `tests/touch-css.test.ts` keeps the gate in place. HIG controls also get no tap highlight, `touch-action: manipulation`, no long-press selection, `:active` press feedback, 16px text fields on touch, overscroll containment, and safe-area padding on edge sheets and dialogs.
- Native market-direction colours: `useColors()` adds `up` and `down` (defaulting to `buy`/`sell`), `HIGProvider marketColors` overrides them and is inherited, and `NumberReadout`, `Usd`, `Percent` and `AnimatedNumberReadout` accept `tone="up" | "down"`. New types: `HIGMarketColors`, `HIGMarketColorRole` and `HIGTableColorRole`. `colorTable()` and `useBrandColors()` now return the table roles only. This replaces the mobile app's local patch of 0.2.0.
- Native `AnimatedNumberReadout` hides its underlying reading with clear white (`#ffffff00`) instead of `transparent`. Android Fabric reads `0x00000000` as unset and drew that reading in black under the reels.
- Motion: web button presses ease over 160ms on the standard curve instead of snapping. `AnimatedNumberReadout` uses the standard HIG curve (`tokens.easing.standard`) on web and native. Native digit reels mount 20 rows instead of 30, a third less to render per animated place.
- Native test consumers track Expo SDK 58.0.23, which expects `react-native-safe-area-context ~5.11.1`, and the peer range now accepts it.
- Accept `@bloxwap/chart` 0.4.0: the required peer is now `^0.3.0 || ^0.4.0`, and HIG develops, smoke-tests and documents against 0.4.0. Chart 0.4.0 only adds indicator APIs, so `PriceChart` and `chartTheme` are unchanged.
- Pin `@bloxwap/font` 0.1.4. Its web CSS now loads unicode-range subsets, so pages download only the scripts they render. The variable sources behind `@bloxwap/hig/native-fonts` and `@bloxwap/hig/og-fonts` are byte-identical to 0.1.2, so the generated TTFs and their SHA-256 hashes are unchanged; only the manifests' source version changes.
- The docs site now installs `@bloxwap/haptic` 0.3.0 and `@bloxwap/sfx` 0.2.1, inside HIG's supported peer ranges, instead of the older 0.1.0 builds.
